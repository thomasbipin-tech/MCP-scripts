Flagbound

The Shifting Battlefield

Steal the red flag from the far base and carry it back to your own. Your flag must be home for a capture to count.

Finding it: a tall pink beam marks the red flag from anywhere — just walk toward it. The tag on screen always shows the direction and distance. You are the round one with the gold gem spinning overhead.

Skills: Q dashes you forward, E fires a grapple at whatever you are looking at and reels you in, and R in mid-air slams down with a shockwave that flattens everyone nearby. Hold Space while falling to glide — worth remembering when the ground disappears.

Sixty seconds in, the battlefield breaks apart. Everything you are standing on comes loose and falls, dropping you into the glowing deep below. Your flag comes with you if you are carrying it.
