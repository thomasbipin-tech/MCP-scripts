Flagbound

The Shifting Battlefield

Steal the crimson flag from the far base and carry it back to your own. Your flag must be home for a capture to count.

Sixty seconds in, the ground breaks apart. Everything you are standing on shatters into falling blocks and drops you into the kingdom underneath, where the battle continues. Your flag comes with you if you are carrying it.
